WELCOME TO GROUNDSKEEPER

A clearer day in the field.

  1. Plan the day. Build a suggested day plan, review the jobs, crews and route, then apply it.
  2. Price with a reason. Inspect labor, travel, equipment, supplies and overhead. Review a suggested quote before saving it.
  3. Keep the crew moving. Start and finish visits in Crew view, confirming actual services and times.
  4. End on a good note. Review completed job values and download customer-message drafts.
This workspace uses the supplied mock customer workbook. Street maps use OpenStreetMap and forecasts use Open-Meteo. Coordinates come from the sheet and are not independently verified. Routes follow the car-road network using OSRM. Driving times are estimates without live traffic. Live GPS and email delivery are not connected. Edits are saved to your signed-in business account.
